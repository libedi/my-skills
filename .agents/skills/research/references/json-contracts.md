# JSON contracts for structural checks

These are minimal machine-readable fields used by `scripts/validate.py`. Other fields from the design may be added without changing the validator. The examples in the integrated design use YAML for readability; runtime files use JSON.

`state.json` is a single object:

- `research_id`, `plan_version`, `original_request`, and `units`.
- Each Unit has `id`, `subproblem_id`, `required_questions` (objects with `id`, `importance`, `sufficient_when`), `axes` (objects with `id`, `question`, `required_question_ids`), `coverage_plan` (objects with `question_id` and `primary_axis`), `question_coverage` (objects with `question_id`, `status`, `claim_ids`), `result_status`, `stop_reasons`, `unknowns`, and `objections`. Before the report stage, result-related fields may be absent.
- For a complex request add `parent_questions` (objects with `id` and `importance`) and `required_outcomes` (IDs), `subproblems` (objects with `id`, `unit_id`, `parent_question_ids`, `required_outcome_ids`, `execution_status`, `result_status`, `validity`), `global_coverage` (objects with `question_id`, `primary_subproblem_id`, `outcome_ids`, `status`, `claim_ids`, `report_location`), and `global_result` (status, stop reasons, unknowns, objections) before reporting.
- An objection includes `id`, `severity`, and `resolution`. A blocking objection is resolved only by `accepted` or `rejected_with_evidence` with a documented resolution reason; if accepted, the associated conclusion must be revised or withdrawn. The validator checks structure, not whether the revision is semantically sufficient.

`ledger.json` has `research_id`, `units` as a mapping of Unit IDs to objects with `limits` and `entries`, and `request_usage.consistency_work_items`. An entry has `id`, `round`, `kind` (`explore`, `extra_explore`, `verify`, `critic`, `retry`, `status_check`), `target`, and `state` (`reserved`, `dispatched`, `finished`, `failed`, `cancelled`). A cancelled entry can be released only when `dispatched_at` is null and `release_reason` is nonempty. The default limits are 3 initial, 2 rounds, 2 supplementary, 1 extra Explorer, 1 Critic, 1 retry, 5 investigation tasks, and 1 Status Check. Custom limits may be lower. Higher limits require `authorized_limit_override: true` and a nonempty `authorization_reference` on that Unit; the validator checks the recorded authorization reference, not whether a user actually granted it. The request-wide consistency allowance is at most 2. To reserve, add the proposed entry, run the dispatch check, then dispatch and record time. Never reserve a batch against stale ledger state.

A full Explorer `result.json` has `research_id`, `unit_id`, `axis_id`, `plan_version`, `status`, `answer_summary`, `claims`, `evidence`, and `question_coverage`. Claims need `id`, `statement`, `kind`, `question_ids`, `supporting_evidence_ids`, `contradicting_evidence_ids`, `premise_claim_ids`, `confidence`, and `confidence_reason`. Evidence needs `id`, `source_type`, `locator`, `location`, `origin_id`, and `observation`. Coverage entries need `question_id`, `status`, and `claim_ids`. IDs must be unique and use the `<research-id>/<unit-id>/<axis-id>-C<number>` or `-E<number>` pattern. The validator checks references and structural evidence for facts, not whether cited evidence actually supports them. Keep source date/revision, applicability, assumptions, limitations, search log, alternatives, hypotheses, and unknowns when relevant.

A `report` validation checks both state and ledger. It rejects `complete` when critical coverage is not answered, a blocking objection remains unresolved, a required outcome is missing at global level, or an essential Subproblem is stale. `partial` and `needs_user_decision` require at least one stop reason and linked unresolved Unknown. The script emits JSON with `ok`, `errors`, and `warnings` and exits nonzero on structural errors. Keep the emitted result in the run record. It does not evaluate factual truth, independence of source origins, quality of axes, sufficiency of evidence, or correctness of user-choice classification.
